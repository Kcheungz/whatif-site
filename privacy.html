Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What-If Studio ("the app") is a personal tool that publishes our own videos to our own social media accounts. This policy explains what data it handles.

What the app accesses

When we connect one of our accounts (YouTube, Instagram or TikTok), the platform gives the app an access token and basic profile information (such as the account name), plus the permission to upload videos to that account. For TikTok this is limited to the user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish permissions.

How it is used and stored

  • Tokens and account names are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private local file. They are never sent to any server other than the platform they belong to.
  • They are used only to upload our videos and to show which account a video will be posted to.
  • We do not sell, share or analyse this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Viewers and other users

The app does not collect any information about people who watch our videos, and no one other than the owner can use the app.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from each platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token file also removes the app's access.
