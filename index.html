What-If Studio

What-If Studio is the private publishing tool behind Waatiff Videos, a channel of short animated "What if…?" science videos (for example, What if all the world's bees disappeared?).

Each video is written, animated and rendered by us. What-If Studio then uploads the finished video to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, with a caption and cover image, so we don't have to post each one by hand.

The tool is used only by its owner, only for our own accounts. It does not offer sign-up, does not post for anyone else, and does not collect data from viewers.
